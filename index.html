Horizontal overflow detected

A child element is wider than its available space, causing the page to scroll sideways.

LIKELY CAUSE
width: 1200px;

A fixed width can exceed a smaller viewport. The element cannot shrink to fit the screen.

SUGGESTED FIX
max-width: 100%;
box-sizing: border-box;
